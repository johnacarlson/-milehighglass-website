**Objective:** Build a Notion workspace for content management, team coordination, and metrics tracking. This is the operating system for executing 10x/day content strategy.

**Deliverable:** Live Notion workspace with 5 databases ready for use by Jack, Lee, Marina, and Editor:
1. Content Calendar (70 posts/week organized by pillar, status, filming dates, post dates)
2. Clip Library (raw footage organized by pillar, duration, filming date, status)
3. Weekly Editorial Tracker (progress toward 70 clips: filmed %, edited %, scheduled %)
4. Metrics Tracker (weekly follower count, engagement %, saves, Shopify traffic, pre-orders)
5. Team Assignments (roles, capacity, contact info, status for Jack, Lee, Marina, Editor, Ambassadors)

**Notion Setup Details:**

**Database 1: Content Calendar**
- Properties: Title, Pillar, Status, Assigned To, Filming Date, Post Date, Content Type, Hook, CTA, Footage Link, Edited Video Link, Performance Notes
- Purpose: Central hub for all 70 posts per week; used for planning, filming coordination, editing tracking, scheduling, and performance analysis
- Template included for consistency

**Database 2: Clip Library**
- Properties: Title, Pillar, Raw Footage Link, Duration, Date Filmed, Notes, Status
- Purpose: Store and organize raw footage for quick reference during batch editing

**Database 3: Weekly Editorial Tracker**
- Properties: Week, Total Clips Needed, Clips Filmed, Clips Edited, Clips Scheduled, Filming % Complete (formula), Editing % Complete (formula), On Track (checkbox), Notes
- Purpose: Weekly sprint tracker; at-a-glance view of progress toward 70 clips

**Database 4: Metrics Tracker**
- Properties: Date, Follower Count, Engagement Rate %, Avg Views per Post, Saves Rate %, Shopify Link Clicks, Waitlist Signups, Pre-Orders, Top Performing Pillar, Top Performing Post, Notes
- Purpose: Weekly metrics review; track growth trajectory, identify top-performing content types, spot trends for next week adjustments

**Database 5: Team Assignments**
- Properties: Team Member, Role, Weekly Capacity (hours), Specialization, Contact, Status
- Purpose: Quick reference for team roles, capacity, and availability
