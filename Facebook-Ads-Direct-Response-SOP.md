# Facebook Ads Direct Response SOP
## Ninefold Consulting Performance Media Framework

**Based on:** Cashvertising (Drew Eric Whitman) + John Carlton copywriting principles  
**Purpose:** Systematic lead generation for contractors, blue-collar businesses, and info product creators  
**Goal:** Lower CAC, higher-quality leads, predictable ROI

---

## PART 1: CASHVERTISING PRINCIPLES (Chapter Summaries + Application)

### Core Insight: You're Not Selling — You're Interrupting Attention & Promising Benefit

Cashvertising teaches that successful ads do three things:
1. **Interrupt** with emotional resonance (not cuteness or cleverness)
2. **Engage** by addressing a specific pain or desire
3. **Conclude** with a clear, irresistible action

Facebook's algorithm amplifies this: ads that spark clicks, comments, and shares = cheaper reach. Ads designed for response (not brand awareness) generate these signals naturally.

## PART 2: THE FIVE ELEMENTS OF A DIRECT RESPONSE FACEBOOK AD

### 1. THE HOOK (Headline + First Line of Copy)

**What it does:** Stops the scroll. Gets the click. Creates curiosity or recognition.

**Cashvertising principle:** The headline must make a specific promise or acknowledge a specific problem. Generic doesn't work.

**Writing framework:**
- **Problem acknowledgment:** "Roofing contractors are losing $2K-5K per job to slow scheduling"
- **Specific result:** "Solar installers who do THIS see 3x more qualified leads in 30 days"
- **Curiosity + benefit:** "The one thing every HVAC company gets wrong (and how it's costing you $50K/year)"
- **Forbidden knowledge:** "Why Google Ads is wasting your money (and what contractors use instead)"

**Testing rule:** Rotate 3-5 different headline angles. The winner usually reveals what your audience actually cares about.

**Avoid:**
- Generic: "More Leads Faster"
- Vague: "You won't believe this"
- Fake urgency: "Limited time only" (on every ad)

**Example for roofing:** "How a roofing contractor in Dallas tripled his pipeline without spending more on ads"

---

### 2. THE BODY COPY (First 2-3 Lines Visible Before "See More")

**What it does:** Deepens the hook. Builds credibility. Creates the desire to click.

**Cashvertising principle:** "Every sentence must earn its place." No fluff. Every line moves toward the action.

**Structure:**
1. **Acknowledge the pain** (one sentence, conversational)
   - "You're probably running Facebook ads, spending $2K-5K a month, and wondering why your lead quality has tanked."

2. **Introduce the mechanism** (what's different, why it works)
   - "Here's what we found: contractors who pre-qualify prospects using a specific sequence are getting 40% fewer tire-kickers."

3. **Build proof** (if space permits)
   - "We've done this with 47 roofers, HVAC crews, and solar installers in the last 18 months."

4. **Create curiosity toward CTA**
   - "The process takes 15 minutes to set up, and most contractors see results in 2-3 weeks."

**Tone:** Conversational, specific, benefit-driven. Sound like Jack (contractor/operator audience), not a marketer.

**Avoid:**
- Excessive punctuation!!!
- ALL CAPS WORDS
- Salesy language ("Act now," "This is your sign," etc.)

**Example:**
"Most contractors we talk to are doing Facebook ads wrong. They're targeting too broad, they're not qualifying leads before they call, and they're burning cash on tire-kickers. Here's how we fixed it for 20+ trades in the last year — we built a simple qualification system that costs $0 to implement. Most guys see clean leads drop 40%+ inside 3 weeks."

---

### 3. THE IMAGE OR VIDEO ASSET

**What it does:** Stops the scroll before the headline even registers.

**Cashvertising principle:** The image must reflect the benefit or the pain point. It's not decorative—it's functional.

**For contractors:**
- **Before/after** (the most powerful format)
  - Before: Cracked/leaking roof → After: Perfect shingles. Results are irrefutable.
  
- **Real customer testimonial** (face + credibility)
  - Actual contractor/business owner. No stock photos. Authenticity = higher CTR.

- **Problem visualization**
  - Damaged HVAC unit, flooded basement, broken door. Shows the pain your audience feels.

- **The result in context**
  - Happy customer on phone, calendar full of jobs, crew working.

**Technical specs:**
- Square format: 1080x1080px (safest)
- 16:9 landscape: 1200x628px (if video)
- Text overlay: Max 20% of image (Meta's guideline, but less = better CTR)
- File size: <1MB

**Video assets (higher ROI):**
- 15-30 seconds max
- Silent first 2 seconds (captions required)
- Opens with the transformation (hook)
- Shows before/after or problem/solution
- CTA at 3-5 second mark

**Avoid:**
- Stock photos (screams inauthentic)
- Multiple people/busy compositions (scrolling users won't parse it)
- Text-heavy images (use copy instead)
- Cropped testimonials (full-face is stronger)

---

### 4. THE CALL-TO-ACTION (Button + Destination)

**What it does:** Removes friction. Makes the next step obvious.

**Cashvertising principle:** The CTA should match the offer. "Sign up for webinar" isn't the same as "Get a free audit." Specificity = higher conversion.

**Best CTAs for contractors:**
- "Learn More" → Lead magnet or intro call (lowest friction)
- "Get Free Audit" → Qualification form (if you're filtering)
- "Book a Call" → Calendar link (high-intent only)
- "See How" → Case study or demo (social proof)
- "Get Started" → Application form (self-qualifying)

**Rule:** Match the CTA to where the prospect is in their journey.
- **Cold traffic:** "Learn More" or "Get Free [Specific Thing]"
- **Warm traffic (retarget):** "Schedule a Call" or "Apply Now"
- **Hot traffic (previous leads):** "Book Your Audit" or "Claim Your Spot"

**Button placement:** Primary CTA button on the ad itself. Optional secondary CTA in the headline or body.

**Example chain:**
- Ad CTA: "Get Free Lead System Audit"
- Landing page copy: Frames the audit as a $1,000 value, 15 minutes
- Form asks: Company name, # of crew members, current monthly ad spend
- Autoresponder: Sends audit results + 1 free consultation

---

### 5. THE LANDING PAGE (Where The Click Leads)

**What it does:** Converts the click into a lead or customer.

**Cashvertising principle:** Don't send people to your homepage. Send them to a focused page that mirrors the ad's promise.

**Structure:**
1. **Headline (must match ad message)**
   - Ad says: "How to cut lead costs by 40%"
   - Landing page says: "The Contractor's Lead Cost Checklist"

2. **Hero image/video** (reinforces the benefit)
   - Real results, before/after, or the mechanism

3. **Problem + solution** (one section, 50 words max)
   - Acknowledge the pain, introduce your method

4. **Social proof** (if you have it)
   - Case study, testimonial, or outcome metric
   - "47 contractors in Dallas saved $60K+ in ad spend using this method"

5. **The form** (minimum viable friction)
   - Name, email, phone (if you're calling)
   - For low-commitment offers: just name + email
   - For paid/high-commitment: add questions that qualify

6. **CTA copy above form**
   - "Get your personalized audit" (creates anticipation)

7. **Autoresponder**
   - Immediately deliver what you promised
   - Set expectation for next step

**Avoid:**
- Sending to homepage
- Multiple CTAs (focus = higher conversion)
- Asking for too much info upfront
- Generic "Learn More" pages

**Example flow for roofing:**
- Facebook ad: "Why 60% of roofing ads waste money (and the fix)"
- Landing page: "The Roofing Ad Audit" with a form
- Form asks: Company name, email, service area
- Thank you page: Immediate PDF (the audit) + calendar link for 15-min call
- Email: Audit + case study of similar contractor

## PART 3: THE CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE

### Account Setup (Foundational)

1. **Pixel Installation**
   - Install Meta Pixel on website (or use CAPI if high-volume)
   - Track page_view, lead, purchase events
   - Without this, you're flying blind on ROI

2. **Audience Segmentation**
   - **Cold traffic:** Broad targeting (location + interest)
   - **Warm traffic:** Website visitors, email list
   - **Hot traffic:** Previous customers, high-intent leads

3. **Conversion Tracking**
   - Define what "lead" means: form submission, call, qualified opportunity
   - Track cost per lead, lead quality (close rate), revenue per lead

### Campaign Types & When to Use

**Type 1: Lead Generation (Low-ticket offer)**
- **Goal:** Build list, establish authority, generate qualified leads
- **Best for:** Service-based businesses, info products, consulting
- **Budget:** $500-2K/month to start
- **Timeline:** 30-60 days to optimize
- **Offer:** Free audit, checklist, webinar, consultation call
- **Example:** HVAC contractor running "Free HVAC System Check Audit"

**Type 2: Sales (Direct response)**
- **Goal:** Immediate revenue, low-friction purchase
- **Best for:** Digital products, courses, low-ticket services
- **Budget:** Variable based on AOV
- **Timeline:** 14-21 days to find winners
- **Offer:** $47-$297 digital product, course, done-for-you service
- **Example:** Info product creator selling $97 course on lead generation

**Type 3: Retargeting (Warm audiences)**
- **Goal:** Convert warm traffic that didn't convert first time
- **Best for:** All businesses (complement to cold traffic)
- **Budget:** 20-30% of total ad budget
- **Timeline:** Ongoing
- **Offer:** Same offer (stronger message), upgrade offer, or social proof angle
- **Example:** Site visitor saw audit offer but didn't click → Retarget with testimonial

### Ad Creation Workflow

**Week 1: Create & Launch**
1. Write 3-5 headline variations (addressing different pain points)
2. Create 2-3 asset variations (before/after, testimonial, problem, result)
3. Write 2 body copy variations (short pain acknowledgment vs. longer story)
4. Set up 1 landing page (can iterate based on CTR)
5. Launch with 3 ad sets ($15-30/day each, test for 3-5 days)

**Week 2-3: Analyze & Optimize**
- Kill winners with CTR <1.5%, CPC >$3
- Scale winners (ads with CTR >2.5%, CPC <$1.50)
- Increase budget on ad set getting cheapest leads
- Note: Don't optimize on landing page performance yet (wait for 50+ leads)

**Week 4+: Scale or Iterate**
- If winners exist: increase daily budget 10-20% every 3-5 days
- If no winners: change creative, targeting, or offer
- Start A/B testing landing page copy (after you have 100+ leads)
- Begin retargeting website visitors and leads who haven't converted

### Budget Allocation (Rule of Thumb)

**Starter campaign ($2K/month):**
- $1,200 cold traffic (lead gen or sales)
- $400 retargeting (existing audience)
- $400 testing new creative/audiences

**Scaled campaign ($5K+/month):**
- 50% cold (proven campaigns)
- 25% warm (retargeting)
- 25% testing (new creatives, offers, audiences)

## PART 4: COPY FRAMEWORKS FROM CASHVERTISING & CARLTON

### Framework 1: Problem-Mechanism-Proof-Action (PMPA)

**Problem:** Acknowledge the specific pain
- "Most contractors lose 60% of their leads to competitors because they don't respond in the first hour."

**Mechanism:** Introduce your solution/method
- "Here's the system we built: a qualification workflow that auto-responds to leads and books calls before your competitor even sees the inquiry."

**Proof:** Show it works
- "47 contractors using this system saw 40% more qualified appointments in 90 days."

**Action:** Clear, specific next step
- "Get your free lead system audit — we'll show you exactly where you're losing deals."

---

### Framework 2: The Story (Before-After-Bridge)

**Before (the pain is real):**
- "6 months ago, Marco was spending $3K/month on Facebook ads, getting 20 leads, but only 2-3 were actually qualified. He was exhausted."

**After (transformation with your system):**
- "After implementing our lead qualification process, he's now getting 15-20 qualified leads at half the cost. His team went from overwhelmed to actually busy with good deals."

**Bridge (how they did it):**
- "It wasn't complicated. He did three things differently in how he structured his ad campaign and follow-up."

**Action:**
- "You can do this too. Let's audit your current system and show you exactly what's working (and what's not). Get your free audit here."

---

### Framework 3: The Forbidden Knowledge

**Setup:** Create curiosity/intrigue
- "Why Google Ads is costing contractors 3x more than Facebook Ads (and nobody talks about it)"

**The secret/insight:**
- "Google targets people actively searching for your service — but they're also the most price-sensitive. Facebook targets people who aren't even thinking about your service yet — but they buy based on recommendation and social proof, not price."

**The mechanism:**
- "That's why our best-performing campaigns blend both, but structure them differently. Google gets the high-intent traffic. Facebook builds authority and gets the best ROI."

**Action:**
- "We've documented exactly how we do this for 40+ trades. Get the free breakdown."

---

### Framework 4: The Metric Hook

**Lead with a specific number:**
- "Roofers using this system cut their cost-per-lead from $150 to $42."
- "HVAC contractors see 340% ROI in the first 90 days."
- "Solar installers generate 200+ qualified leads per month at under $30 each."

**Why it works:** Metrics are credible. They're specific. They remove abstract benefit claims.

## PART 5: WHAT NOT TO DO (Mistakes That Kill ROI)

### Copy Mistakes
1. **Generic headlines** ("More Leads," "Grow Your Business")
   - Fix: Specific benefit + mechanism

2. **Excessive punctuation & emojis** 
   - Fix: Clean, professional, conversational tone

3. **Making it about you** ("We're the best")
   - Fix: Make it about the prospect's outcome

4. **No clear offer** (Ad doesn't say what they get)
   - Fix: "Get your free X" or "Book your consultation"

5. **Mismatched CTA** (Ad says "webinar" but landing page asks for credit card)
   - Fix: Match the promise throughout the funnel

### Targeting Mistakes
1. **Too broad** (Targeting all of US for a local service)
   - Fix: Geographic radius + affinity interests + behaviors

2. **Too narrow** (Targeting only exact match interests, no reach)
   - Fix: Start broad, let algorithm optimize after 50+ conversions

3. **Ignoring warm audiences** (Only running cold traffic ads)
   - Fix: Always retarget website visitors and leads

4. **Not segmenting by intent** (Same ad to cold + warm + hot audiences)
   - Fix: Different messaging for different stages

### Campaign Mistakes
1. **Optimizing too fast** (Killing ads at 10 conversions)
   - Fix: Wait 50+ conversions before assessing performance

2. **Setting budget too low** (Running $5/day ads)
   - Fix: Minimum $15-20/day per ad set (Meta needs volume to optimize)

3. **Not tracking conversions** (No pixel, no form tracking)
   - Fix: Install pixel day one, track everything

4. **Changing creative every 3 days** (Never letting winners compound)
   - Fix: Scale winners for 2-4 weeks before testing new creative

## PART 6: YOUR WEEKLY WORKFLOW

### Monday: Plan & Launch
- Review last week's performance
- Identify what worked (CTR >2%, CPC <threshold)
- Create new variations (2-3 new creatives or copy angles)
- Launch new ads or scale winners
- Budget: Allocate to winning ad sets (+10-20%)

### Wednesday: Mid-week Check
- Look at leading indicators: impressions, clicks, CTR
- Pause ads with CTR <1% and CPC >$3
- Note: Don't judge conversion yet (needs 50+ leads)

### Friday: Analysis & Planning
- Full performance review (weekly ROI, cost per lead, lead quality)
- Document what worked (copy angle, creative type, audience)
- Plan next week's creatives
- Update client with results

### Monthly: Strategic Review
- Analyze which ad types generate best customers (close rate + LTV)
- Plan for retargeting improvements
- Test new offer or messaging angle
- Adjust budget based on CAC vs. LTV

## PART 7: METRICS THAT MATTER

**Don't optimize for:**
- Reach (vanity metric)
- Impressions (doesn't tell you if ads worked)
- Click-through rate alone (clicks without conversions = waste)

**DO optimize for:**
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Total ad spend ÷ leads generated
  - Target: <$50 for most services; <$100 for enterprise services
  
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):** Total ad spend ÷ customers acquired
  - Target: 3-5x less than customer lifetime value
  
- **Lead Quality (%):** Percentage of leads that qualify or convert
  - Target: 30-50% of inquiries become opportunities
  
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Revenue from ads ÷ ad spend
  - Target: 3:1 minimum; 5:1+ is strong
  
- **Conversion Rate on Landing Page:** Visitors who submit form ÷ total visitors
  - Target: 15-30% (depends on offer friction)

## PART 8: SCALING THE WINNING FORMULA

**When you've found a winner:**

1. **Increase budget slowly** (10-20% every 3-5 days)
   - Aggressive scaling often breaks the algorithm

2. **Keep winning creatives fresh** (rotate every 4-6 weeks)
   - Same message, different assets (new photo, different angle)
   - Prevents ad fatigue

3. **Expand cold audience targeting** (same location, adjacent interests)
   - If HVAC ads work in Dallas, test Austin, Houston with same creative

4. **Build your warm audience** (retarget harder)
   - Warm traffic converts 2-4x better than cold
   - Allocate 30-40% of budget here once you have 5K+ website visitors

5. **Test new offers** (same audience, new lead magnet)
   - If "Free Audit" works, test "Free Consultation" or "Free Masterclass"
   - New offer = fresh campaign momentum

## QUICK REFERENCE: 30-DAY CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK

**Days 1-3:**
- Set up pixel, tracking
- Write 5 headlines, 2 ad copy angles, create 3 assets
- Build landing page
- Launch 3 ad sets, $15-30/day each

**Days 4-7:**
- Review CTR, pause underperformers (<1.5% CTR)
- Scale winners 10-15%
- Begin gathering initial performance data

**Days 8-14:**
- Analyze first 50+ conversions
- Identify winning copy angle & creative type
- Pause ads with high CPC/low conversion
- Launch retargeting campaign to website visitors

**Days 15-21:**
- Scale winning ad set by 20%
- Create new creative variation (same message, different asset)
- Analyze lead quality (which leads actually convert?)

**Days 22-30:**
- Full analysis: ROI, CAC, lead quality
- Plan month 2: scale winners, test new offer or audience
- Brief client/stakeholder on results and next steps

## FINAL NOTES

**Direct response copywriting applied to Facebook:**

You're not trying to build brand awareness. You're trying to get a specific person to take a specific action today. Everything—from the headline to the landing page—should laser-focus on that one outcome.

The best ads for contractors and info product creators are the ones that sound like someone who actually understands the business talking to someone who runs the business. No corporate fluff. No buzzwords. Just clear, specific, benefit-driven copy.

Start small, test relentlessly, scale ruthlessly.
