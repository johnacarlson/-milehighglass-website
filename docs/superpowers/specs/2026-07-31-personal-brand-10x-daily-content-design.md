# Personal Brand 10x/Day Content Strategy — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Project:** Jack Carlson personal brand (Instagram) → Hawthorne lifestyle funnel  
**Goal:** Build massive personal brand through 10x/day content; let Hawthorne sell implicitly through lifestyle

---

## 1. Overview

**Primary Goal:** Establish Jack Carlson as a worth-following personal brand across all life dimensions (business building, fitness, learning, discipline, brand leadership).

**Secondary Goal:** Naturally funnel followers to Hawthorne through implicit lifestyle integration (not hard-sell, just living it).

**Success Metric:** Follower count + engagement rate (target: 50K followers + 8-10% engagement in 60 days).

**Timeline:** Start posting immediately; 10 posts/day, 70 posts/week sustained.

---

## 2. Content Pillars & Integration

All content flows from Jack's actual life. No compartmentalization — everything is integrated under the **"Stat Grind" narrative** (video game character maxing out stats across all life dimensions).

### **Fitness & Discipline (15-20 posts/week)**
- Run club participation (clips of runs, communities, people)
- Weight gain/eating journey (food content, gym clips, body progress)
- Sobriety check-ins and lifestyle (living sober narrative)
- Daily Optimization Routine / Stat Grind Protocol (the Christian Bale Batman-inspired discipline protocol, rebranded and on-camera)

### **Business & Learning (15-20 posts/week)**
- Marketing principles and frameworks (actionable tips, concepts broken down)
- Copywriting and behavioral science (book clips, one-liners, applications)
- Reading recommendations (current books, key takeaways, "what I'm reading")
- Business-building in real-time (what Jack is doing operationally, strategies being tested)

### **Hawthorne Lifestyle (10-15 posts/week)**
- Brand aesthetic and culture (lifestyle clips that embody Hawthorne DNA)
- Behind-the-scenes design and building (limited drops, creative process, limited editions)
- Ambassador content (Ryan, Raf, IG girl blonde — authentic moments)
- Brand storytelling (faith elements underplayed but present, rebellious positioning, underground/archive vibe)

### **Personal & Momentum (10-15 posts/week)**
- Vulnerability and story (bits about fear, things Jack didn't think he could do)
- Progress and wins (traction on business, brand, personal goals)
- Aura/presence (moments that show Jack as a leader worth following)
- Life lessons and observations

**Total: ~70 posts/week = 10/day**

## 3. Execution Model

### **Team**
- **Jack:** Creative direction, on-camera talent, daily life activities
- **Lee (Filmer):** Captures B-roll during key moments (runs, gym, Hawthorne sessions, daily life)
- **Marina (Second Brain):** Strategic input on content angles and positioning
- **Ambassadors:** Ryan, Raf, IG girl (blonde) — guest content, authentic perspectives
- **Editor:** Batch edits 70 clips weekly (can use CapCut AI or hire a person)

### **Weekly Workflow**

**Monday–Friday (Filming):**
- Jack films naturally during daily activities (phone camera for quick clips)
- Lee shadows key moments and captures 15-30 second B-roll segments
- Accumulate raw footage across all pillars: fitness, business, Hawthorne, personal

**Saturday or Sunday (2-3 Hour Batch Edit):**
1. Dump all 70 raw clips into editing workspace
2. Add captions, text overlays, hooks (keep it simple; CapCut AI can template most of this)
3. Ensure each clip has a clear hook (first 3 seconds grab attention)
4. Add CTAs naturally (no hard sell; "swipe this," "building this," "limited pieces," etc.)
5. Schedule all 70 posts for the week in Meta Business Suite or Buffer

### **Production Quality**
- **Phone camera footage:** Prioritize authenticity over polish. Raw, real moments perform better than highly produced content.
- **B-roll from Lee:** Professional but natural. Lifestyle shots, not staged ads.
- **Editing:** Captions, color grading, 2-3 second clips stitched together. No complex effects. Speed and consistency > perfection.
- **Audio:** Trending audio, needle drops, or just natural sound. Keep it dynamic.

## 4. Content Calendar Structure

Posts flow from Jack's actual schedule, not a pre-planned grid. However, to maintain consistency:

### **Daily Distribution (Example of 10 Posts/Day)**
- 2-3 fitness/discipline clips (morning routine, run club, eating, gym)
- 2-3 business/learning clips (marketing tips, copywriting, book moments)
- 1-2 Hawthorne lifestyle clips (design, ambassador moments, brand vibe)
- 1-2 personal/momentum clips (progress updates, vulnerability, aura moments)
- 1 wildcard (trending topic, reactive content, timely observation)

**Posting cadence:** Spread 10 posts across 12-16 waking hours (roughly every 1.5-2 hours). This maximizes algorithm reach without spamming followers.

### **Weekly Themes (Optional, Not Required)**
- **Monday:** Momentum recap from weekend
- **Tuesday–Thursday:** High-frequency business + fitness mix
- **Friday:** Hawthorne focus + weekend preview
- **Saturday–Sunday:** Lifestyle, personal, brand building

## 5. The Implicit Hawthorne Funnel

### **No Hard Sell**
- Hawthorne is **not** a sales pitch in the content. It's part of Jack's lifestyle.
- Posts about brand building, design process, limited drops, ambassador moments — all authentic, no conversion optimization.

### **Link in Bio**
- Shopify page (Jack builds this today/tomorrow) accessible via link in bio.
- Pre-order waitlist and first drop (20 sweatpants + 20 crewnecks) available for anyone who finds the link.
- No aggressive CTAs per post; the link exists if people want it.

### **Implicit Conversion Path**
1. Follow Jack for business + fitness + lifestyle content
2. See Hawthorne as part of what he's building and living
3. Become curious about the brand ("What's Hawthorne? He keeps showing it.")
4. Check link in bio → land on Shopify
5. Join waitlist or pre-order (self-selected, high-intent buyers)

### **Scarcity as Organic Mechanic**
- Limited drop (40 pieces) that sells out creates real FOMO
- Sell-out story becomes social proof for drop 2
- No restocking = authenticity, not theater

## 6. Success Metrics

### **Primary (Track Weekly)**
- **Follower growth:** Target 50K in 60 days (from current baseline)
- **Engagement rate:** 8-10% across all posts
- **Save rate:** High saves indicate valuable, revisit-worthy content

### **Secondary (Track Monthly)**
- **Shopify traffic:** Link in bio clicks, landing page visits
- **Hawthorne pre-orders:** Waitlist signups, first drop sales
- **Viral moments:** Posts that hit 50K+ views (signal what resonates)

### **Vanity (Nice to Have, Don't Optimize)**
- Comments, shares, DM inquiries (track qualitatively)
- Brand mentions outside of Instagram (Reddit, Twitter, forums)

---

## 7. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Unsustainable to film 70 clips/week | Lee captures B-roll during key moments; phone clips are quick. Total filming time ~5-10 hours/week. |
| Editor bottleneck | Use CapCut AI templates to batch-edit faster, or hire a part-time editor ($500-1000/month). |
| Content feels forced or salesy | Anchor everything in Jack's actual life. If it's not real, don't post it. Quality >> frequency. |
| Algorithm rewards change | 10x/day creates enough surface area that you'll find what works. Consistency compounds over time. |
| Hawthorne pre-orders don't convert | That's okay. The primary goal is personal brand reach. Pre-orders are a natural downstream effect, not the driver. |

## Appendix: Stat Grind Narrative

The overarching frame that ties everything together: Jack is a video game character grinding to **max out his stats** across all life dimensions.

**Stats being leveled:**
- **Business Acumen** (marketing, copywriting, operations, strategy)
- **Wealth** (making money, building revenue streams, investing)
- **Fitness** (strength, endurance, body composition, discipline)
- **Intelligence** (reading, learning, behavioral science, systems thinking)
- **Discipline** (daily protocol, consistency, showing up)
- **Brand Authority** (Hawthorne, personal presence, influence)

Every piece of content is a level-up in one or more of these stats. The narrative gives coherence to what might otherwise look like random life content.
